## How to buy a commercial license

Email **hp@cumulativeweb.com** with:

1. Who you are (name, company).
2. What you want to do with KingCode Lens.
3. Roughly how many users / devices it will touch.

Commercial licenses are negotiated per deal and may include production
support, custom eyewear or companion-app integrations, and IP
indemnification — agreed in writing, never implied.

## FAQ

**Is there a free trial for companies?**
Yes — Evaluation Use is free. Internal testing and non-revenue demos to
decide on a purchase need no permission and no payment.

**Can I fork it on GitHub?**
GitHub forks for personal evaluation and development are fine (that's how
the platform works). Republishing the code elsewhere, mirroring it, or
distributing copies to third parties is not permitted without our written
permission. Linking to the official repo is always welcome.

**Can I contribute?**
Yes — bug reports and PRs are welcome. By contributing you agree your
contribution is licensed under the same terms (see LICENSE §6).

**Why not open source?**
KingCode Lens is a commercial CWI product. We keep the source public so
anyone can evaluate it honestly, and we keep the rights so the business
that builds it can survive. That's the whole model.

**Who owns it?**
Cumulative Web Inc. All intellectual property is retained by CWI (LICENSE
§5). The software is licensed to you, not sold.
